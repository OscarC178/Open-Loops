Open Loops

Everything you've asked people for, tracked until they reply — then chased, in your own words.

Open Loops watches the messages you send — on Slack and by email — and keeps a list of every request that hasn't been answered yet. When someone replies, it tells you it's your turn. When they don't, it writes the "just checking in…" nudge the way you'd write it.

  • Runs on your computer. No servers, no accounts, no analytics — a small local app.
  • Uses the AI subscription you already have. Claude or Grok; plug in either.
  • Drafts by default. It never sends anything unless you explicitly switch sending on.
  • Open source. Read every line before you trust it with your inbox.
